# quadpod crawl gait sketch — 28 October 2026

New folder `firmware/spyder_esp32_ble_crawl` (BLE `Spyder-Crawl-BLE`, v1.0): the walking sketch with `Walk.cpp`, `Walk.h` and `WalkConfig.h` replaced by the step sequences of [hpsaturn/quadpod](https://github.com/hpsaturn/quadpod/blob/master/src/servos.cpp) (SunFounder crawling-robot lineage). Existing sketches are unchanged, following the owner rule to add walking code as new folders.

## Design evidence

- **Lift order.** A static-stability sweep over all 24 lift orders on Spyder's stance found quadpod's forward and back orders tied for best. Its left and right orders are among the best. This confirms the leg mapping (quadpod 0 FR, 1 RR, 2 FL, 3 RL → Spyder 1, 3, 0, 2).
- **Stance and margin.** Spyder's front/rear-station stance is long and narrow, and gives only ~5 mm stability margin unmodified. quadpod's square stance gives ~17 mm. A lean before each lift (all feet down) restores ≥12 mm.
- **Lift height.** Default ±30° knee/foot limits cap lift at about 10 mm at the 85/−60 mm stand. 20 mm (quadpod's) reaches ~37–43°.
