## Coverage

- Reused controller: isolated per-joint calibration, bounds/strict commands, direction/reference checks, inverse kinematics checked by independent forward kinematics, supported standing/partial extension, finite cycles and complete-path preflight.
- Timing: STOP holds and cancels, OFF releases, fractional speeds, delayed-loop step cap and millisecond wrap.
- Record codec: CRC/version/semantic validation, all256 individual bit corruptions, output-off-only application.
- Three-channel hardware adapter: all16 channels full-off at initialization; only0–2 may activate; one joint can detach without changing another; global release inhibits OE before I2C cleanup; disconnect at the attach boundary prevents re-enabling; I2C errors latch disabled.
- Actual-sketch host harness: USB calibration/poses/step and stop/release; source-specific Bluetooth status replies; valid saved calibration loads at boot without activation; detached-only save/load; failed storage and corrupted records; UART errors and Bluetooth RX overflow hold/cancel; disconnect releases every joint; later per-joint activation does not wake released channels; write faults disable the whole leg.

The new runner first failed because the ESP32 leg adaptation was absent. Reused controller/parser checks then passed against the new copies; adapter and actual-sketch checks failed until their corresponding implementation was added. Previous one-motor overflow handling was retained through an application-owned bounded Bluetooth queue and UART receive-error callbacks.

## Limits and physical checks

Host doubles substitute I2C/GPIO, UART, Bluetooth and scheduler boundaries. They do not reproduce real FreeRTOS scheduling, execute the reply worker, establish radio pairing/loss/congestion timing, measure PWM oscillator accuracy, validate reset/OE electrical behavior, assess supply performance or prove mechanical clearance/balance. The independent reviewer explicitly left these physical/concurrency properties unverified.

The leg retains its earlier Servo-library degree scale (**90° requests1472us**), unlike the separate one-motor tester's nominal90°=1500us. Its hard electrical envelope remains1000–2000us, initial limits60–120°, absolute allowed45–140°. No full-range sweep is provided for an assembled leg.

Use the included wiring/calibration README. In addition to confirmed SDA21/SCL22, connect OE toGPIO23 and fit a pull-up that holds it HIGH during reset. A board's OE pull-down can defeat a10k pull-up; the README explains the confirmed10k-pull-down/1k-pull-up case and requires voltage verification with servo power off. Use separate suitable servo power with common ground; keep the motor supply off for upload/reset.

The estimated27.5/55/74mm geometry and pose values were reused, not newly measured.
